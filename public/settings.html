🔑 How to get your Kaggle Username & API Token:
  1. Log into your account at kaggle.com.
  2. Go to your Account Settings page (kaggle.com/settings).
  3. Scroll down to the API section.
  4. Click Create New Token — this downloads a file named kaggle.json.
  5. Open kaggle.json to copy your "username" and "key" into these boxes, then click Save Settings.
